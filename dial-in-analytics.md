## Summary & watch-list

- **One-and-done retention is the biggest issue.** 82% of users haven't returned after their first brew. Push notifications, streak mechanics, and badge-driven re-engagement are the main levers already built in.
- **Two power users are carrying volume.** The 39-brew user alone is ~52% of all brews. Understanding what brought them back is worth a direct conversation.
- **Anonymous-to-registered conversion is low (19%).** Most users brew without leaving an email — making it hard to reach them. The save-your-results prompt after tasting is the primary nudge.
- **Pour-over dominates; espresso is underdeveloped.** 78% pour-over vs 12% espresso despite espresso being the product's centrepiece. May reflect who found the app so far rather than a product gap.
- **June 8 spike has not been sustained.** Worth identifying what drove that week (a post, a review, a share) and replicating it.
- **Pro monetisation not yet active.** 0 of 27 users are paying. This is expected pre-launch but is the critical next milestone.
