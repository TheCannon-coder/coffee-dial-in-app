## 1. Diagnosis — espresso pulling too fast

Card: 01.svg

Caption:
Shot gushing out fast and tasting thin and sour? Grind finer. One step.

Fast means the water found an easy way through the puck. Make that way a little harder and it slows down. I'm a café owner in Hamilton and this is still the first thing I reach for. Change only the grind, then taste it.

## 2. Diagnosis — moka pot bitter

Card: 02.svg (light)

Caption:
Bitter moka pot? Take it off the heat at the first sputter.

That gurgle is the coffee telling you it's done. Everything after it is scorching. Lower the flame, pull the pot the second it sputters, and run the base under cold water if you want it to stop for sure. One change, then see what the cup says.
