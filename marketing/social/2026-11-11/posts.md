## 1. Diagnosis — French press muddy

Card: 01.svg

Caption:
Muddy, silty French press? Grind coarser. That's the whole fix.

Fine grounds slip through the mesh and keep extracting in your cup, so it gets heavy and bitter at the bottom. I'm a café owner in Hamilton and I've poured a lot of sludgy cups. Go one step coarser, leave the steep time alone, and taste again.

## 2. Diagnosis — Chemex stalling

Card: 02.svg (light)

Caption:
Chemex taking forever and tasting bitter? Try a touch coarser.

The filter is thick, so a fine grind clogs it and the water just sits on the coffee too long. Coarser lets it drain, and the bitterness usually eases with it. One change, then see what the cup says.

#chemex #chemexcoffee #pourover #coffeetips #coffeebrewcoach

Alt text: Cream card with the label CHEMEX and the text "Chemex stalling and tasting bitter? Grind coarser. A fine grind clogs the thick filter and the water sits too long. Go a touch coarser."

Why this week: different method from post 1, and pour-over is where most current brewers are (brief §3).
