Bitter moka pot? Nine times out of ten it's the heat, not the coffee.

## 2. Diagnosis — espresso pulling too fast

Card: 02.svg (light)

Caption:
Shot running fast and tasting thin and sour? Grind finer. One step, not three.

Fast means the water found an easy way through the puck. Making the way a little harder slows it down, and a small move keeps you from overshooting. Change only the grind, then taste it. I do this every morning and it's still the first thing I reach for.

#espresso #espressotips #homebarista #pullingshots #coffeebrewcoach

Alt text: Cream card with the label ESPRESSO and the text "Shot running too fast? Go finer. Just one step. Fast and thin means the water found an easy way through. Make the way harder."

Why this week: "espresso pulling too fast" / "shot pulling too fast" are the top espresso queries, and the new-machine owner is the primary persona.
