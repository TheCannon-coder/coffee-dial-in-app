## 1. Diagnosis — cold brew tastes weak

Card: 01.svg

Caption:
Cold brew tasting weak and watery? Add more coffee before you add more time.

Steeping longer pulls out more bitter stuff, not more of the good stuff. If the batch is thin, the fix is usually more grounds for the same water. Change only that, then taste it tomorrow. Make a small batch first :)

## 2. Diagnosis — V60 draining slow and bitter

Card: 02.svg (light)

Caption:
V60 dripping slow and tasting bitter? Go one notch coarser.

When the water stalls at the end, it's sitting in grounds that are too fine, and it keeps pulling bitterness out. I've watched a lot of drawdowns, and a slow finish is the first thing I check. Change only the grind, nothing else, and see what the cup says.

#v60 #pourover #coffeetips #homebarista #coffeebrewcoach

Alt text: Cream card with the label V60 and the text "Draining slow and tasting bitter? Go a little coarser. A stall past the finish means the water is stuck in fine grounds. Open it up one notch."

Why this week: V60 grind queries show up repeatedly, and V60 is the method most of our brewers actually use.
