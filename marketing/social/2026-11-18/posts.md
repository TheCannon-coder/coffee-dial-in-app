## 1. Diagnosis — drip machine thin or sour

Card: 01.svg

Caption:
Drip machine coffee tasting thin, flat or a bit sour? Grind a touch finer.

If the grind's too coarse the water rushes straight through and doesn't pull much out. Aim for something like beach sand. I'm a café owner in Hamilton, and this is the first thing I check when someone says their home coffee is "just okay." One change, then taste again.

#dripcoffee #coffeemaker #coffeetips #homebarista #coffeebrewcoach

## 2. Diagnosis — Kalita Wave sour

Card: 02.svg (light)

Caption:
Kalita Wave tasting sour and a bit hollow? Try one step finer.

The flat bed is forgiving, but if the grind's too coarse the water leaves before it's done its job. Go a step finer, keep everything else the same, and see what the cup says.

#kalitawave #pourover #coffeetips #homebarista #coffeebrewcoach

Alt text: Cream card with the label KALITA WAVE and the text "Kalita cup tastes sour and hollow? Grind a step finer. The flat bed drains fast and forgiving. If it's sour, the water left too soon. Slow it down."

Why this week: different method from post 1; Kalita hasn't appeared before.
