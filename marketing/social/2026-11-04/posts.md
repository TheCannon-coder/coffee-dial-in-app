## 2. Diagnosis — AeroPress bitter

Card: 02.svg (light)

Caption:
Bitter AeroPress? Try 15 seconds less steep time.

A long steep keeps pulling out the harsh stuff after the good stuff is already in the cup. Shorten the steep, keep the grind and dose the same, and see what the cup says. One change at a time is the only way you'll know what did it.

#aeropress #aeropressrecipe #coffeetips #homebarista #coffeebrewcoach
